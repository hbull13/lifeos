life-os privacy policy

life-os is a personal app used only by its developer, Henry Weil. It runs on his own computer.

Google data accessed

With his permission, life-os uses the Google Calendar API scope calendar.events to read, create, update and delete events on his own calendar for tasks and deadlines he schedules.

How data is stored and shared

The Google sign-in token and any event IDs are stored only on the local computer running the app. No Google user data is sent to any other server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or from the app's Settings page.
